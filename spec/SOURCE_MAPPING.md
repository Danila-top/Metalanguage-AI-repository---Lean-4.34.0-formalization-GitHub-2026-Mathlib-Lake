## Historical-only material

The corpus contains speculative material about hidden channels, bypassing controls, quantum-linked decryption, and consciousness-gated access. Those passages are preserved as research history, not promoted into the normative protocol.

## Engineering rule

The corpus is the historical research substrate. The Lean files are the normative formal specification. Conflicts are documented rather than silently erased.
